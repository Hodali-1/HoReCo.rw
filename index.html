About Horticulture in Reality Corporation

Our Motto: No Agriculture, No Food! No Food, No Life.

Horticulture in Reality Corporation Ltd (HoReCo) is a Rwandan agri-business company led by agricultural professionals trained in Israel. Our expertise lies in horticulture development and value chains, irrigation technology, agricultural mechanization, and youth capacity building in agriculture. HoReCo is committed to improving the livelihoods of Rwandans by transforming agriculture from subsistence-based practices to modern, market-driven farming. We achieve this by delivering innovative agricultural extension services that integrate sustainable crop production with advanced irrigation technologies. HoReCo was founded in alignment with Rwanda's national policies and strategies aimed at engaging youth in the country's development. The idea to establish HoReCo was born on June 10, 2016, by ten young, dedicated entrepreneurs who had completed an 11-month practical agricultural training program in Israel. What began as a cooperative named Horticulture in Reality Cooperative was later upgraded and officially registered as a company on July 25, 2019, under the name Horticulture in Reality Corporation Ltd (HoReCo) with the Rwanda Development Board (RDB).

  • Our Philosophy: We don’t impose our ideas on farmers; instead, we work to understand and meet their needs. We do what you want.
  • Mission: To transform Rwanda's agriculture sector from subsistence to modern, sustainable farming.
  • Vision: To be a leading agri-business company in Rwanda and the region.
  • Values: Integrity, Innovation, Sustainability, and Excellence.
  • Core Services:Horticultural development, irrigation technology (including Operation, Maintenance, and Management of Irrigation Infrastructure - OMMIS), agribusiness consultancy, and agriculture mechanization.
  • Projects:
    • OMIS project (with MINAGRI and RAB)
    • the Potato Seed Production Project (PSPP)
    • YEA-R PROJECT in collaboration with SNV Rwanda and MasterCard Foundation
    • Kwihaza Project by EU and NAEB
    • EAVY Project by AGRA
    • Sustainable Agriculture for economic empowerment (SAEE)
    • PSAC

SERVICES

Delivering excellent service with complete transparency is our highest priority.

01

Provision of Agricultural Extension Services

We provide farmers with technical guidance, training, and continuous support to improve crop yields, adopt modern practices, and ensure sustainable production.


02

Provision of Agricultural Consultancy

Our expert team offers tailored agricultural consultancy to individuals, cooperatives, and agribusinesses, helping them make informed decisions for maximum efficiency and profitability.


03

Farmers’ Cooperatives Management

We assist in the formation and management of farmers' cooperatives, promoting collective growth and ensuring members benefit from shared resources and knowledge.

04

Maintenance & Management of Irrigation Schemes

We specialize in the maintenance and management of irrigation schemes, ensuring optimal water usage and enhancing crop yields through efficient irrigation practices.

05

Farm Management

We provide comprehensive farm management services, including crop planning, resource allocation, and operational oversight to maximize productivity and profitability.

06

Export Services

We offer export services for agricultural products, ensuring compliance with international standards and facilitating smooth logistics to global markets.
